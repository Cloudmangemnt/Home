Privacy Policy

How this publication handles basic visitor information.

Overview

Cloudwise Journal is a static informational website. In this supplied version, the site does not use sign-in accounts, contact forms, advertising scripts, analytics tags, tracking cookies, or a subscriber database.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider (for example, GitHub Pages) may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver the site and protect its infrastructure. Review the hosting provider’s privacy information for details.

External resources

The design loads fonts from Google Fonts. When the browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical request information. External links, if later added, are subject to their own privacy policies.

Future changes

If the owner adds analytics, ads, embedded services, or a contact form, this policy must be reviewed and updated before those features are enabled.

Contact

Use the site’s Contact page for privacy inquiries once a public editorial contact channel is configured.
