Editorial Disclaimer

A note on scope, independence, and technical guidance.

No managed cloud services

Cloudwise Journal is an informational blog, not a cloud-management service provider. Publishing practical guides does not constitute an offer to manage or operate readers’ systems.

Verify before you deploy

Examples and recommendations may not suit your environment. Changes to production systems should follow your own review, testing, security, backup, and approval processes.

Provider names

Names such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Terraform, and Kubernetes are used for commentary and education. Their trademarks belong to their respective owners. No endorsement or affiliation is implied.

Corrections and transparency

We welcome specific, verifiable corrections and should disclose paid placements if introduced in the future.
